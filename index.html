03 / THE BIG IDEA

Stop looking for
the steering wheel!

Prolog isn't Python with strange punctuation. It's a different way to think about programming: describe facts, relationships and constraints — then ask what follows.

This is an open, growing book in English. Every chapter is free to read, and the interactive exercises run in your browser. No signup. No fuss. Just curiosity.
